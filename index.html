Website · Woods at Sasan · 2026 — Present

The Woods at Sasan website

A CMS-based site for a five-star property, designed with two freelance designers I briefed and led. Built on Framer, with the booking engine, analytics and WhatsApp connected behind it.

  • 11x+Blended ROAS
  • ₹20L+Business generated
  • ~30Bookings, direct and sales-assisted
  • 1.54LWebsite sessions
How it was built
  • Benchmarked Indian hotels, a direct competitor and Michelin Key hotels worldwide to decide which pages the site needed.
  • Briefed and led two freelance designers from first brief to launch.
  • Built on Framer, and connected the booking engine, analytics, tag manager, Meta Pixel and WhatsApp single-handedly.
  • Treated launch as the start of the work, not the end of it.

App design · Glii · 2022 — 2023

Glii, a queer dating app

I designed the app's screens, and the words and flow behind them. Glii was designed queer-first, so that it feels safer for the community it serves.

  • 90+User-facing screens designed
  • 5Marketing channels for launch
  • 6%Increase in repeat engagement
Safety, by design
  • Dates booked at neutral locations, restaurants or cafes, directly through the app. Reservation via Zomato was a feature that did not exist in competing apps.
  • An integrated SOS button.
  • Several parameters for background screening.
  • Flagging of irrelevant profiles.
